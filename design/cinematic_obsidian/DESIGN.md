## Brand & Style

The design system embodies immersive cinematic authority, drawing viewers directly into compelling visual storytelling. Built for high-engagement, ambient media consumption, it pairs stark obsidian environments with vibrant, high-energy accents.

The design philosophy combines **Dark-Mode Cinema Modernism** with crisp architectural precision:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep near-black and charcoal layering simulates a darkened theater environment, prioritizing edge-to-edge imagery, video billboarding, and visual media over interface chrome.
- **Focus & Contrast:** The UI steps back; bright high-contrast whites and punchy red accents guide user focus toward active playback, curated hero carousels, and high-priority calls to action.
- **Tone & Demeanor:** Sophisticated, immediate, and premium. Interactions feel responsive, cinematic, and deliberate.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, light-absorbing environment where media key art takes center stage.

- **Primary Accent (`#E50914`):** The signature electric crimson. Reserved strictly for dominant actions—primary call-to-action buttons, active playback scrubbers, live-state badges, and key selection rings.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#141414`):** The default backdrop tone. Supported by layered surfaces ranging from `#0F0F0F` (base underlay) to `#181818` (standard media tile surface) and `#232323` (popovers, active drawer overlays, and floating toolbars).
- **Secondary Tone (`#2D2D2D`):** An industrial slate used for secondary button fills, passive borders, and carousel progress tracks.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#192247`):** A midnight navy tone utilized sparingly for atmospheric vignette gradients, specialty genre tagging, and contextual backdrop ambient glows.
- **Typography & Icons:** High-contrast `#FFFFFF` for primary titles, labels, and critical indicators; muted `#A3A3A3` for metadata, durations, genre lists, and inactive states.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Geist** across all roles to achieve a modern, geometric, and high-legibility interface. Its condensed proportions and clean apertures make it ideal for metadata strings, numeric badge alignments, and bold cinematic billboards.

- **Headlines:** Display and Headline roles employ tight letter-spacing and heavy weights (700–800) to replicate movie title treatments and prominent billboard descriptions.
- **Body:** Body text prioritizes effortless scanning against pitch-black backgrounds, using neutral weights (400) and open line-heights to eliminate optical strain.
- **Labels & Badges:** Labels make use of medium-to-bold weights with slight positive letter tracking for maturity ratings (e.g., "TV-MA"), audio specs (e.g., "Dolby Vision", "4K HDR"), and timestamp stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts to screen density, shifting between responsive fluid containment on mobile and edge-bleeding cinematic horizontal rows on desktop:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a 12-column grid for standard browsing pages, featuring outer canvas margins of `3.5rem` and gutters of `1.5rem`. Media carousels break outside container bounds to bleed toward the right viewport edge, inviting horizontal exploration.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.5rem` to `1rem` gutters. Carousels utilize snap-scrolling touch physics with half-tile edge reveals to cue lateral discovery.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strict incremental spacing units (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) maintain consistency between media cards, section category titles, and metadata locks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system avoids physical drop shadows in favor of tonal gradation, directional scrims, and ambient light physics:

- **Backdrop Scrims & Vignettes:** Hero showcases and billboard assets utilize directional linear and radial gradients (e.g., `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(20,20,20,0) 0%, rgba(20,20,20,0.8) 70%, #141414 100%)`) to guarantee seamless text contrast without harsh containment boxes.
- **Tonal Stacking:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas):* `#0F0F0F`
  - *Tier 1 (Surface/Grid Layer):* `#141414`
  - *Tier 2 (Interactive Tiles & Flyouts):* `#181818` with subtle 1px border highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
  - *Tier 3 (Floating Controls & Navigation):* `#1F1F1F` or frosted glass (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop-blur).
- **Scale Elevation:** Media cards elevate primarily via physical transform scaling (e.g., `scale(1.05)`) on hover/focus, backed by an ambient, deep-tinted halo shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural curvature profile using **Soft** roundedness (`1`):

- **Media Posters & Cards:** Standard cards and thumbnail containers use subtle corner treatment (`0.25rem` / `4px`) to preserve raw cinematic crop aesthetics without looking childish or overly bulbous.
- **Interactive Badges & Tooltips:** Use `0.25rem` corners to enforce sharp, tabular efficiency.
- **Buttons & Pills:** Primary action buttons feature standard soft rounding (`0.25rem`), while quick-filter tags and floating mobile action items use fully curved pill forms to signal interactive tactile affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Play Button:** Solid white background (`#FFFFFF`) with dark near-black typography (`#0F0F0F`), high visual priority, bold font weight, icon-text gap `space-xs`.
- **Secondary Action (More Info / Add to List):** Translucent charcoal background (`rgba(109, 109, 110, 0.4)` or `#2D2D2D`) with pure white text and crisp vector iconography.
- **Destructive / Accent:** Solid `#E50914` background with pure white typography, utilized for subscribe, resume playback, and promotional actions.

### Horizontal Content Carousels
- Edge-to-edge touch or arrow-paginated rows with category title aligned above.
- Poster aspect ratios: `16:9` for video episodes, `2:3` for theatrical key art, and `1:1` circular/rounded thumbnails for character/actor rows.
- Interactive hover/focus state expands the tile above neighboring items, revealing immediate contextual playback controls and meta information (match percentage, maturity rating, runtime).

### Navigation Bars
- **Desktop Header:** Fixed transparent top bar that transitions into frosted solid `#141414` with a gentle backdrop blur on vertical scroll. Houses logo, primary category links, search icon trigger, and profile avatar dropdown.
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar:** Persistent dock fixed to the viewport bottom. Dark charcoal surface (`#121212`) with 1px top border line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). Features icon-above-label layout, tinting active items with `#FFFFFF` or `#E50914` and inactive items with `#A3A3A3`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact height (`32px`), pill-curved silhouettes (`9999px`), styled with dark translucent slate fills and subtle ghost borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- Active state fills with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and solid border.

### Input Fields
- Understated input boxes with deep background (`#1C1C1C`), recessed border (`1px solid #333333`), and white input text. Focus state transitions the border to pure white or signature red (`#E50914`) with no glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox borders use `#A3A3A3`, filling with `#E50914` and a white check icon upon selection.
- Switches utilize smooth pill slides with charcoal tracks transitioning into red upon activation.
